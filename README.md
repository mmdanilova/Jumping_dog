
# Jumping dog

## Main idea
A logic-developing game based on the principles of dynamic programming. The goal is to maximize energy savings while progressing from level to level.

## Instruction
To jump from one platform to another, simply click on it. You can jump to an adjacent platform or over one. Jumping to an adjacent platform will cost you |y2-y1| energy, while jumping over a platform will cost you 3.|y3-y1| energy (y is the platform's height). You initially have 2500 energy units; the goal is to spend the least amount of energy and reach the finish line. If your result deviates from the ideal by more than 25%, you must restart the level.
The meaning of each button:
1. The first number next to the lightning bolt icon represents your current energy level.
2. The coins you have in your account.
3. If the button is green, you can currently buy a hint; pink means the opposite.
4. The last button is used to turn the game on and off, making it easier to complete levels.
